Document Type: Master Franchise Agreement, Section 4.2 (Returns & Refunds)
Applies To: All Think9 portfolio brands
Last Updated: 2025-11-01

All Think9 portfolio brands must offer customers a minimum return window of
15 days from the date of purchase, with refund to the original payment
method, in order to maintain consistent consumer trust across the portfolio.
Brand-specific exceptions require written sign-off from the Group Legal team.
